How do I…?

Go live

1. Start your live stream on YouTube.

2. On YouTube, press Share and copy the link.

3. Paste it under Live stream on this page and press Show it on the website.

4. When you finish, press Stop showing it.

Approve a testimony

New stories appear under Testimonies waiting for you. Watch the whole video first. Press Approve to put it on the website, or Decline to remove it. Nobody else can see a story until you approve it.

Change something else on the website

Write down what you want changed, with the exact words, and send it to your helper. It shows up on the website a minute or two after they make the change.

The website is not working

1. Press Check again at the top of this page.

2. If the light is still red, open any other website to check your own internet.

3. If only the ministry website is down, contact your helper and tell them when you noticed.

Someone says they sent a prayer request and I never got it

Check the Spam folder first. The prayer page opens the person's own email app, so if they closed it without pressing Send, nothing was sent. Ask them to try again, or to email the ministry directly.

I got an email saying a payment failed or the domain is expiring

Do not click links in the email. Open Porkbun from the button on this page and look there. If Porkbun shows a problem, update the payment card there. If it shows nothing wrong, the email was a scam and you can delete it.

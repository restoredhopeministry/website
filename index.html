Next steps

I prayed. Now what?

If you just reached out to God — maybe for the first time, maybe for the first time in years — here's how to keep walking.

Know that He heard you

You don't need special words. Scripture says whoever calls on the name of the Lord shall be saved (Romans 10:13). That includes you.

Keep talking to Him

Prayer is just honest conversation. Tell God the truth about your day, out loud or in your head. Two minutes in the morning is a real start.

Open the Bible

Start with the Gospel of John. Read one chapter a day and ask one question: what does this show me about Jesus?

Tell one person

Healing happens in the light. Tell someone you trust what you're carrying and what you prayed.

Find a church

You weren't meant to do this by yourself. Look for a church that teaches the Bible and makes room for people who are hurting. Ask the pastor about baptism.

Get the help you need

Faith and practical help work together. A counselor, a doctor or a recovery group can be part of how God restores you.

For the one helping

How to pray with a hurting friend

You don't need to be a pastor. You need to show up, listen and bring them to Jesus.

Before you pray

  • Listen first. Let them finish. Silence is fine.
  • Ask, "Can I pray with you right now?" and respect a no.
  • Ask what they'd like prayer for instead of guessing.

When you pray

  • Keep it short and plain. Thirty seconds is enough.
  • Use their name. Say back to God what they told you.
  • Pray one promise over them, like Psalm 34:18.

Say this

  • "I'm so glad you told me."
  • "I'm not going anywhere."
  • "That sounds so heavy. Thank you for trusting me with it."

Skip this

  • "Just have more faith" or "just pray about it."
  • "Everything happens for a reason."
  • "Others have it worse."
  • Fixing, lecturing, or making it about your own story.

Afterward

  • Check in tomorrow. A text that says "praying for you today" matters.
  • Keep what they said private.
  • Help them find the next step: a church, a group, a counselor.

If they mention suicide, take it seriously. Ask directly: "Are you thinking about killing yourself?" Asking does not plant the idea. Stay with them, and call or text 988 together. If they are in immediate danger, call 911.
